The appendix offers supplementary materials to support the content featured on the Aurora website. It includes standardized references, acronyms, and a shared lexicon of terms designed to promote clarity and consistency across departments. These resources give readers a convenient reference for aligning technical, operational, and strategic discussions under a unified vocabulary, and point to external sources that inform Aurora's design and governance.

- **[Acronyms](/team/appendix/acronyms/)** — a quick-reference list of common acronyms used throughout the Aurora documentation.
- **[Lexicon](/team/appendix/lexicon/)** — shared definitions of key Aurora terms and concepts.
